twin-local privacy policy

twin-local is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: Gmail messages (read), Gmail drafts (create), Google Calendar events (read), for the owner's account only.
  • Use: preparing summaries and draft replies for the owner. The app never sends email.
  • Storage: processed and stored only on the owner's computer; credentials are kept in the OS keychain.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or AI model training.
  • Revoke access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
